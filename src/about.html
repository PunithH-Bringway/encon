About Encon

An environmental engineering company delivering economical, dependable water and wastewater solutions from Bengaluru.

Corporate Profile

Turnkey expertise in water & recycle

We are an experienced turnkey solution provider in the field of water, sewage and wastewater treatment & recycle systems, including zero liquid discharge (ZLD).

Together with our sister company ADENPRO Environmental Consultants, we provide the most comprehensive, economical solutions to treatment and recycle requirements for industrial, institutional, domestic and municipal customers through a pioneering approach. Our one-stop model covers design, process engineering, civil & structural design and construction, electro-mechanical supply, troubleshooting, refurbishing, retrofitting and maintenance.

Our team has delivered work for Bangalore International Airport, Himatsingka Linens, Thermax, Indian Design Exports and many more, and has contributed to the design of plants as far afield as a 10 MLD sugar industry ETP in Ethiopia.
