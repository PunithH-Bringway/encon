Treatment Technologies

The right process for your effluent

We select from proven biological technologies for both STP and ETP, matched to your load, footprint and discharge norms.

Sequencing Batch Reactor

Fill, react, settle and decant in a single tank, so equalisation, aeration and clarification share one footprint.

  • Compact, ideal where space is tight
  • Handles fluctuating flows well
  • Excellent nutrient removal

MBBR / FBBR

Free-moving plastic media carry a dense biofilm, packing far more treatment capacity into each cubic metre of tank.

  • Easy upgrade of existing plants
  • Robust against shock loads
  • Low sludge generation

ASP / Extended Aeration

The proven, conventional workhorse: suspended growth with fine-bubble diffused aeration and secondary clarification.

  • Simple, well-understood operation
  • Stable effluent quality
  • Suited to large municipal flows

Membrane Bio-Reactor

Biological treatment coupled with membrane separation delivers reuse-grade water without a secondary clarifier.

  • Highest treated-water quality
  • Smallest footprint
  • Direct feed to RO for recycle
